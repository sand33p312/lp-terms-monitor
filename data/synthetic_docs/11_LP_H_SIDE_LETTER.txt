ABC PRIVATE EQUITY FUND IV
SIDE LETTER — INVESTOR LP-H
SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT — NOT A LEGAL AGREEMENT
Execution date: 12 February 2025
Effective date: 1 January 2025

Clause 4.1 — Most favoured nation
If the General Partner enters into a side letter with any other Limited Partner on terms more favourable than those set out in this letter, Investor LP-H shall be offered the benefit of those terms.

Clause 4.2 — Management fee
The management fee payable by Investor LP-H shall be reduced to one point five per cent (1.5%) per annum of its Commitment.

Clause 4.3 — Portfolio company audit reports
The General Partner shall provide Investor LP-H with a copy of the audited financial statements of each Portfolio Company within thirty (30) calendar days after the General Partner receives those audited financial statements.

Note: Only Clause 4.3 is a reporting obligation. Its deadline runs from an event (receipt of accounts), not from a period end.
